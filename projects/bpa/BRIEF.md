# BPA — Stride

Stride is a Business Professionals of America (BPA) preparation site for students and advisors. It organizes practice by event and offers a separate workspace for presentation and project preparation.

## Goals and constraints

- Let guests go straight to tests and skills. Guest work is temporary and clears on reload or exit. Signed-in users can save their own progress.
- Use official event references and authorized past papers. Label original practice and unsupported event coverage clearly.
- Keep Stride as the primary site, preserve its scroll-driven landing animation and red, white, blue and black identity, and selectively borrow landing-page colors and objects from the separate BPA Prep Lab project.
