# ISEF — Stock Network Research

A proposed mathematics research project for ISEF on relationships between large technology-stock moves and later moves in other stocks. The owner wants to use roughly ten years of price history and inspect a manageable number of major events.

## Goals and constraints

- Test whether pre-event connections help explain later market-adjusted returns after large gains or losses.
- Use reproducible comparisons and describe findings as associations within the study design.
- Build enough statistics, graph theory and linear algebra understanding to explain and defend the method independently.
