# Conrad — ClearDischarge

Conrad is a student competition project exploring discharge-instruction verification. ClearDischarge demonstrates an independent checker that compares patient-facing discharge instructions with a reviewed clinical source and flags potential discrepancies for clinician review.

## Goals and constraints

- Keep Demo Mode usable without an API key and use only synthetic cases.
- Keep generation and verification independent. A clinician starts discharge preparation and reviews the source, instructions and flags before approval.
- Demonstrate the approach with synthetic examples. Describe prototype evidence without clinical validation or medical-device claims.
