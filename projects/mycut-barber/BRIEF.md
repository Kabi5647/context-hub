# MyCut Barber

MyCut Barber helps people who cut their own hair plan maintenance cuts or style transitions, follow a guided session, and use recorded results for the next haircut.

## Goals and constraints

- Capture hair type, lengths by section, skill level, tools and the intended style.
- Compare measurements with a supported style template and guide trimming, growth or styling by section.
- Use deterministic templates and recorded progress for personalization without paid AI.
