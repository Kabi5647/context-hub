# Drivekind — Driving Practice

Drivekind helps new drivers plan practice sessions with adjustable difficulty, route preferences and progress tracking. The landing page uses a scroll-driven car journey to explain the app.

## Goals and constraints

- Support time-budgeted practice loops and point-to-point alternatives.
- Give drivers control over difficulty and route preferences. Keep demo routes fictional and have users plan while parked.
- Keep authentication data separate from practice history when extending account support. Store password hashes.
